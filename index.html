About

Browser-based real-time transcription for any audio source. Works with police scanners, radio streams, podcasts, or any browser tab playing audio.

  • Whisper AI: Free, runs locally in your browser (~466MB download)
  • Deepgram: Cloud API, excellent accuracy for radio audio ($200 free credit)
  • Privacy: Whisper processes entirely on your device. Enable redaction to hide PII.
